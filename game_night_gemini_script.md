# Game Night — Gemini Art Script

**7 generations total.** Each one is a "sheet" with several items, to save your Gemini limit. I cut them apart, remove the magenta and wire each piece into the 8 smartboard games.

**Before you start:** attach one image from the last set, such as the Gus the Gator companion or the Gold Laurel frame, and say:
> "Match this exact art style for everything in this chat: clean bold dark outlines, soft cel shading, rich saturated colors, chunky playful proportions, glossy game-show finish. It should feel like a premium mobile game that teenagers think is cool, not babyish."

**Paste these rules once at the top of the chat:**
> - No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere, unless I specifically ask for a number.
> - Where I say magenta, use solid flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) with no gradient, shadow, glow or texture.
> - Keep every item fully inside its cell, with wide magenta gaps between items. Nothing touches or overlaps, and nothing is cut off at the edges.
> - No real brands, TV-show logos or copyrighted characters.

## Generation 1 — Game icons (8 badges for the Present tab)
> Square 1:1 image on solid pure magenta (#FF00FF). A 4-column × 2-row grid of 8 separate glossy **game-show app icons**. Each is a rounded-square tile with a thick colored border, a soft inner glow, and one bold central symbol that pops out slightly past the tile edge (3D, playful):
> 1. **Category Clash** — royal-blue tile with a 3×3 grid of glowing gold game-show screens and a small golden trophy in front.
> 2. **Territory Takeover** — bright-blue tile with an antique map of colored territories and a planted red flag.
> 3. **Rival Rebound** — orange tile with a basketball swishing through a hoop, motion lines, and a few big chalk "✕" marks flying off.
> 4. **Treasure Chests** — amber tile with an open treasure chest overflowing with gold coins and one sparkling gem.
> 5. **Prize Ladder** — purple tile with a golden ladder climbing toward a glowing star, coins on the rungs.
> 6. **Four in a Row** — red tile with a blue upright game grid and red and gold discs, one disc mid-drop.
> 7. **Summit Squad** — teal tile with a snowy mountain peak, a flag on top and a climbing rope.
> 8. **Live Question** — icy-blue tile with a big glowing ice-crystal question mark and frost sparkles.
