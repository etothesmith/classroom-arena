# Classroom Arena: Avatars, Shop and Escape Room — Gemini Art Script

**8 generations total.** Most are "sheets" (several items in one image) to save your Gemini limit. I cut them apart and remove the magenta background, so the items only need clear magenta space between them.

**Before you start:** attach one image from the Liberty Vault set, such as `artifact_golden_spike.png`, and say:
> "Match this exact art style for everything in this chat: clean bold dark outlines, soft cel shading, rich saturated colors, slightly chunky proportions, game-icon finish."

**Rules that apply to every generation** (paste once at the top of the chat):
> - No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.
> - Where I say magenta, use solid flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) with no gradient, shadow, glow or texture.
> - Keep every item fully inside its cell, with wide magenta gaps between items. Nothing touches or overlaps.
> - Nothing is cut off at the edges.

The game already works without this art: it uses emoji and CSS colors as stand-ins, and swaps in your art automatically once it's uploaded.
